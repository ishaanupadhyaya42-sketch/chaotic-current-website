Robot 01

Patriot

2025–26 Competition Season

Patriot is our rapid-fire scorer, built around a dual-flywheel launcher tuned for consistency over raw speed. After early vision instability, we swapped to a Logitech C920 for reliable AprilTag detection, redesigned the outtake ramp for uniform ball positioning and exit angles, and moved to a 1:1 flywheel gear ratio to hold torque under load. A second motor and third flywheel added complexity but eliminated firing delays entirely.

DrivetrainMecanum, GoBILDA
Scoring systemDual-flywheel launcher, 1:1 gear ratio
VisionLogitech C920, AprilTag detection
Odometry / IMUGoBILDA odometry, REV 9-axis IMU
ChassisACM composite walls

Robot 02

Stinger

2025–26 Competition Season

Stinger is our current competition robot, built from scratch for speed, defense, and full-field range. A 3-section intake pulls in three balls at once through medium-compression REV silicon wheels, funnels them past goBILDA rubber gears, and fires them off two flexible 72mm flywheels stabilized by Swyft inertia weights through a 3D-printed hood angled at 35 degrees. Impact-resistant ACM composite walls protect the electronics without the weight penalty of aluminum, which matters with defense allowed on the field this season.

DrivetrainMecanum, belt-driven front wheels
Intake3-section REV silicon wheel system, 3-ball capacity
Scoring systemDual 72mm flywheels, Swyft inertia weights
VisionLogitech C920, AprilTag detection
ChassisACM composite paneling
