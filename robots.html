Robot 02 · Version 2.0 · current

Stinger

Stinger is our current competition robot, built from scratch for speed, defense and full-field range. A three-section intake pulls in three balls at once through medium-compression REV silicone wheels, funnels them past goBILDA rubber gears and fires them off two flexible 72 mm flywheels, steadied by Swyft inertia weights, through a 3D-printed hood angled at 35°.

Impact-resistant ACM composite walls protect the electronics without the weight of aluminum, which matters with defense allowed on the field this season.

Drivetrain
Mecanum, belt-driven front wheels
Intake
Three-section REV silicone wheels, three-ball capacity
Launcher
Dual 72 mm flywheels, Swyft inertia weights
Vision
Logitech C920, AprilTag detection
Chassis
ACM composite panels

Robot 01 · Version 1.0

Patriot

Patriot was our rapid-fire scorer, built around a dual-flywheel launcher tuned for consistency over raw speed.

After early vision trouble we moved to a Logitech C920 for reliable AprilTag detection, redesigned the outtake ramp so every ball left at the same position and angle, and went to a 1:1 flywheel gear ratio to hold torque under load. A second motor and third flywheel added parts but removed firing delays entirely.

Drivetrain
Mecanum, goBILDA
Launcher
Dual flywheels, 1:1 gear ratio
Vision
Logitech C920, AprilTag detection
Odometry
goBILDA odometry, REV 9-axis IMU
Chassis
ACM composite walls
