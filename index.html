Robot 02 · version 2.0 · current

Stinger, our competition robot

Built from scratch for speed, defense and full-field range. Keep scrolling and it comes apart, part by part, the way we built it.

Exploded view

Intake to launcher in one pass

A three-section intake pulls in three balls at once through medium-compression REV silicone wheels, funnels them past goBILDA rubber gears and fires them off two flexible 72 mm flywheels through a 3D-printed hood set at 35°.

Stinger specs

What it runs on

Drivetrain
Mecanum, belt-driven front wheels
Intake
Three-section REV silicone wheels, three-ball capacity
Launcher
Dual 72 mm flywheels, Swyft inertia weights
Vision
Logitech C920, AprilTag detection
Chassis
ACM composite panels

Chassis

Walls built to take a hit

Impact-resistant ACM composite walls protect the electronics without the weight of aluminum, which matters with defense allowed on the field this season.

Robot 01 · version 1.0

Patriot, the rapid-fire scorer

Our first robot of the season, built around a dual-flywheel launcher tuned for consistency over raw speed.

Exploded view

Four fixes that made it consistent

A Logitech C920 for reliable AprilTag detection, a redesigned outtake ramp so every ball left at the same angle, a 1:1 flywheel gear ratio to hold torque under load, and a second motor with a third flywheel that removed firing delays.

Patriot specs

What it ran on

Drivetrain
Mecanum, goBILDA
Launcher
Dual flywheels, 1:1 gear ratio
Vision
Logitech C920, AprilTag detection
Odometry
goBILDA odometry, REV 9-axis IMU
