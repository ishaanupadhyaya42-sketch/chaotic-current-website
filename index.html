Who we are
Rookies in 2022. Still proving it, three seasons later.

Today we compete at the highest level of the FIRST® Tech Challenge, finishing second at the FIRST in Texas UIL 6A State Championship, the largest and most competitive division in the state, where we served as Finalist Alliance Captain with a perfect qualification record. Along the way we earned the Design Award and climbed into the top four percent of teams worldwide.

But the trophies are only part of the story. Every mechanism, every line of code, and every strategy call is made by students, and the real work is what happens between competitions. We mentor Stiles Middle School's new FTC and FLL teams, and have brought STEM and robot-building lessons to Reagan Elementary, reaching over 75 new FTC/FLL members and 450 students in our community.
