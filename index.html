Robot 02 · current

Stinger, our competition robot

Built from scratch for speed, defense and full-field range. A three-section intake pulls in three balls at once and feeds two 72 mm flywheels through a 3D-printed hood set at 35°.

Drivetrain
Mecanum, belt-driven front wheels
Launcher
Dual 72 mm flywheels, Swyft inertia weights
Chassis
ACM composite panels

Outreach

Teaching robotics in our district

We mentor the new FTC and FLL teams at Stiles Middle School and run STEM and robot-building lessons at Reagan Elementary.

Every mechanism, line of code and strategy call on our own robot is made by students, and we teach younger students to work the same way.
